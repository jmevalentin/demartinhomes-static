Home Equity Planning

Understanding your equity is one of the most useful things you can do when your housing needs are changing.

What is home equity?

Home equity is the part of your home you actually own. In plain terms, it's what your home is worth today minus whatever you still owe on it. If your home could sell for more than your remaining loan balance, that difference is your equity.

Equity isn't cash sitting in an account - it's value tied up in the property. But it's real, and it often becomes a key resource when it's time for your next housing decision.

How equity builds over time

Paying down your mortgage

Each monthly payment chips away at what you owe, and over time a larger share of every payment goes toward the loan balance rather than interest.

Changes in your home's value

If your home's market value rises, your equity can grow even faster. Values can also move the other way, which is why timing and local trends matter.

Improvements you make

Thoughtful updates can add value, though not every project returns what it costs - it helps to know which changes tend to matter in your area.

Why equity matters in a housing transition

When your housing needs change, your equity often shapes what's possible. Here are a few situations where it comes into play.

Rightsizing

When your current home no longer fits your day-to-day life, equity can help you move toward a home that does.

Combining households

If two households are becoming one, understanding the equity in each home helps you plan the transition with clear eyes.

Keep-or-sell decisions

Sometimes the question is whether to sell or hold on to a property. Knowing your equity is part of weighing the options.

Funding a next move

Equity from a sale can become the down payment on your next home, so it often shapes what's realistic and when.

Building a plan around your equity

A housing plan isn't a single decision - it's a picture of where you want to go and the steps to get there. Pauline helps you connect the dots between your equity, your goals, and your timing, so you can move forward with confidence rather than guesswork. Here's what we might look at together:

  • An estimate of your current equity based on today's market
  • The typical costs of selling, so the net picture is realistic
  • Timing - how your goals line up with the market and your life
  • The options in front of you, and how each one might play out

It all starts with knowing what your home is worth today.
