About West Volusia

Objective facts about transportation, recreation, and public services across West Volusia County.

Location & Transportation

West Volusia is served by Interstate 4 and SunRail, with Daytona Beach roughly 30 minutes away and Orlando roughly 45 to 60 minutes away.

Parks, Trails & Nature

The area includes Blue Spring State Park, the St. Johns River, numerous lakes, and multiple county parks and trails.

Schools & Libraries

West Volusia is served by Volusia County Schools, Stetson University in DeLand, and the Volusia County public library system.
